## How to Work Through Both Papers

1. **Start with Study & Understand mode.** No timer — go through Paper 1 topic by topic, reading the solution after every question, right or wrong. This is where the actual learning happens.
2. **Move to a timed attempt for the full paper.** Give yourself a realistic time limit and go through all 60 questions in one sitting, exactly the way you would on exam day.
3. **Use the topic filter to isolate weak spots.** If Mensuration or Simultaneous Equations gave you trouble, re-run just that topic across the paper instead of redoing all 60 questions.
4. **Repeat Paper 2 the same way**, then try both papers back-to-back once you're comfortable — that's the closest simulation of the full BECE Mathematics paper you'll get outside the real exam hall.
5. **Review every attempt the same day.** Go back through both correct and incorrect answers while the question is still fresh — that's when the solution actually sticks.
