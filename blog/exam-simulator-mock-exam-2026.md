## How We Compared These Exam Simulators

We reviewed each platform's official website, pricing and free tier, and compared them on five questions:

1. **Is it genuinely free?** And what is capped behind a paywall?

5. **How close is it to the real exam?** A timed mock, an official format, or just a quiz.

Details change, so confirm current pricing and limits on each platform's website before you commit.

## 9. Anki - Best Spaced Repetition for Long-Term Retention

**Website:** [apps.ankiweb.net](https://apps.ankiweb.net)  
**Price:** Free on desktop (Windows, Mac, Linux) and Android; one-time paid fee on iOS  
**Login required:** No - optional AnkiWeb account for cloud sync  
**Best for:** Long-term retention, medical/law school, languages, any exam requiring deep memorisation

Anki is technically a flashcard app, not an exam simulator - but it earns its place on this list because its spaced repetition system is the most scientifically validated method available for building long-term retention, which is the foundation that all other exam preparation depends on.

Anki shows you a card, you rate how well you remembered it, and the algorithm schedules the next review based on your rating. Cards you find easy are shown less frequently; cards you struggle with come back sooner. Over time, this produces retention rates significantly higher than traditional review methods.

**What we like**
- Spaced repetition is scientifically the most effective memorisation method
- Massive shared deck library covering medicine, law, languages, and more
- Free on desktop and Android

**What could be better**
- iOS app (AnkiMobile) costs a one-time fee
- Dated interface - steeper initial learning curve
- Not an exam simulator - must be paired with a proper mock test platform

**Verdict:** Not a standalone exam simulator, but an essential companion for any exam requiring significant memorisation. Medical and law students should use this alongside their primary mock test platform.

## 10. LawHub - Best Free Official LSAT Practice Platform

**Website:** [lawhub.org](https://lawhub.org)  
**Price:** Free tier (4 practice tests); LawHub Advantage ~\$120/year (58+ tests)  
**Login required:** Yes - LSAC account required  
**Best for:** LSAT preparation with official practice materials from LSAC

> [!NOTE]
> As of June 2024, the official LSAT practice tests and interactive exercises that were previously on Khan Academy moved to LawHub, operated by the Law School Admission Council (LSAC) - the body that actually administers the LSAT.

LawHub provides full-length practice LSATs using the same interface as the actual exam, which matters because the LSAT is a computer-based test with a specific navigation style. Practising in the exact test environment removes one variable of uncertainty on exam day. A free LSAC account is required, but this is the same account you will need to register for the exam itself.

The free tier is genuinely useful but limited - you get 4 full official PrepTests. For serious preparation, LawHub Advantage (\$120/year) unlocks a library of 58+ previously administered official tests. If you have an LSAC fee waiver, LawHub Advantage is available at no cost.

**What we like**
- Official LSAC platform - practice tests are authentic, not third-party approximations
- Same interface as the real exam
- Free account includes 4 full official PrepTests to start
- Updated to reflect the 2024 revised LSAT format

**What could be better**
- Free tier is limited to 4 practice tests - serious prep likely requires LawHub Advantage
- LawHub Advantage costs ~\$120/year (free with LSAC fee waiver)
- Account creation required
- Focused exclusively on LSAT - not useful for other exam types

**Verdict:** If you are preparing for the LSAT, LawHub is now the correct place to go. The free tier gives you 4 official tests to start; budget for LawHub Advantage if you need the full library. Khan Academy no longer hosts the interactive LSAT practice content.

## Final Thoughts: Choosing the Right Exam Simulator or Mock Exam Platform

The best exam simulator for you depends on what exam you are actually taking and how much friction you are willing to accept. Here is a quick decision guide:

- **Competitive banking/government exam in India** → ixamBee or PracticeMock (both require registration)
- **CompTIA certification** → ExamCompass (no login, free) paired with a full-length timed mock exam
- **SAT** → Khan Academy (official partnership, free)
- **LSAT** → LawHub (official LSAC platform, free with account)
- **Custom mock test or teacher-created assessments** → Google Forms or Microsoft Forms, or [ExamOven's custom exam builder](https://examoven.com/create-custom-exam) if you want no login
- **Distraction-free self-study with no account** → **[ExamOven](https://examoven.com/exam-library)** (**Editor's Choice**)
- **Time-per-question analysis and retaking only your mistakes, free** → [ExamOven's results page and Revision Bank](https://examoven.com/blog/how-to-use-examoven-revision-bank)
- **Long-term memorisation for medical or law school** → Anki alongside any other platform
- **Vocabulary or recall-heavy subjects** → Quizlet (free tier is now limited - be aware)

> [!TIP]
> Several platforms on this list advertise free access prominently but require registration before you can take a single test. If you want genuinely zero-barrier practice - no email, no account, no password - **ExamOven** and **ExamCompass** are the only mock-test platforms on this list that deliver that. (Anki also works without an account, but it is a flashcard app, not a mock-test tool.)

Every platform here has at least some free access worth exploring before committing to a paid tier. Pick the one that matches your exam type, run a full timed mock test in your first week of preparation, and build your study routine from there.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which exam simulator does not require a login?

ExamOven is a full timed exam simulator that needs no account, email or password. ExamCompass also needs no login, but it is a question-by-question quiz for CompTIA exams rather than a timed simulator. Anki works without an account too, though it is a flashcard app.

Yes. ExamOven offers free timed mock tests across exams such as IELTS, JEE and NIMCET with no registration, and you can upload your own questions in JSON format. ExamCompass offers free CompTIA practice tests with no signup.

### How much does a mock exam platform cost?

Many options are free. ExamOven, ExamCompass, Khan Academy and Google Forms / Microsoft Forms cost nothing. Quizlet Plus is \$7.99/month or \$35.99/year, and LawHub Advantage is about \$120/year (free with an LSAC fee waiver). ### Which mock test platform has the most detailed analytics?

PracticeMock is strongest on peer benchmarking: All India Rank among 20 lakh+ users, topic-by-topic accuracy and time-per-question data. Full analytics and detailed explanations there are paid, as are Examzify's advanced analytics. ExamOven shows correct, wrong and unattempted counts, time spent on each question, and performance charts for free with no account. It has no peer ranking, because your data stays on your device.

### What does ExamOven show after a practice test?

The results page breaks down your session into correct, wrong and unattempted questions, shows the time you spent on each question, and adds performance charts and an answer review. You can then send your wrong and skipped questions to the Revision Bank to retake or review them.

### Can I retake only the questions I got wrong?

Yes, on ExamOven. The [Revision Bank](https://examoven.com/revision) collects the questions you got wrong or skipped across any set of tests. You can filter by wrong, skipped or both, remove duplicates, then retake them as a timed, scored test or review them untimed with the correct answers and explanations. See the [step-by-step guide](https://examoven.com/blog/how-to-use-examoven-revision-bank).

### Do exam simulators match the real exam interface?

It varies. LawHub uses the same interface as the real LSAT, and PracticeMock's free test closely mirrors the actual exam interface. ixamBee offers format-accurate tests for specific Indian competitive exams. ExamOven simulates timed exam conditions but is not an official replica and is not affiliated with any certification body.

### Does ExamOven track me or lock my browser?

No. ExamOven does not lock your browser, monitor your camera or screen, or track background processes. Your progress is stored in your browser by default, with optional backup and sync to your own Google Drive. Clearing your browser data resets local history unless you have backed it up.

### Can I use my own questions on ExamOven?

Yes. You can upload question sets in JSON format or use the AI-assist feature to generate practice questions. The question banks are open source on [GitHub](https://github.com/Samkarya/online-exam-questions), and you can [request an exam](https://examoven.com/exam-not-found) that is missing.
