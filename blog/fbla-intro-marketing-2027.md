## What's Confirmed for 2026–2027 (and What Isn't)

**Confirmed, based on FBLA's live 2026–2027 competitive events listing:**

- Introduction to Marketing Concepts remains an objective-test-only event, restricted to grades 9 and 10.
- The event description language FBLA publishes for 2026–2027 is essentially unchanged from prior years: foundational marketing principles, product promotion, pricing strategies, consumer behavior, and selling methods.
- FBLA's general "Marketing" event (the grades 9–12 role-play alternative) also continues, unchanged in structure - objective test plus a role-play round for top scorers.

**Not yet independently confirmed by us for the 2026–2027 cycle specifically (verify against the current PDF before relying on it):**

- The exact 50-minute time limit and 100-question count. These have held steady for at least three consecutive program years (2023–2024 through 2025–2026), so a change would be unusual, but we could not pull the literal 2026–2027 PDF text directly to confirm the footer date. Check the PDF footer yourself - FBLA date-stamps every guideline document.
- Any mid-year rule clarifications. FBLA does sometimes issue small updates to guidelines after the initial September release.

> [!TIP]
> The single most reliable way to confirm you're studying current information: open the official guideline PDF, scroll to the bottom, and check the "Updated [Month] [Year]" footer. If it says anything before August 2026, you're looking at last cycle's document.

## The Competition Ladder for 2026–2027

Introduction to Marketing Concepts is contested at up to three levels, depending on your state's structure:

1. **Local chapter** - some chapters run an internal qualifying round before sending competitors up; check with your adviser.
2. **Region/District** (fall/winter, where applicable) - not every state has this layer, and where it exists, dates are set locally.
3. **State Leadership Conference (SLC)** - typically held between January and April 2027, exact dates set independently by each state chapter. There is no single national SLC date.
4. **National Leadership Conference (NLC)** - **June 23–26, 2027, in Columbus, Ohio**, at the Greater Columbus Convention Center. Only competitors who place well enough at their SLC advance here; exact state quotas vary.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Because SLC dates are set state-by-state, there is no way to give one universal "competition date" for this event. Your state FBLA chapter's website is the only authoritative source for your own SLC date. Search "[your state] FBLA State Leadership Conference 2027" once your state posts it, typically in the fall or winter.

## Membership Deadline: What You Actually Need to Know

FBLA's official guidelines state a **national eligibility floor of 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on March 1 of the current program year** - for the 2026–2027 cycle, that's **March 1, 2027**. This is the deadline stated directly in the event's own guidelines document.

Here's the part that trips people up: this is a *floor*, not necessarily *your* deadline. States and even individual district directors can - and often do - set an earlier internal cutoff for their own qualifying conferences. Florida FBLA, for example, requires dues paid by December 1 (or an earlier date its district director sets) to compete at the district or state level. If you wait until March 1 assuming that's universal, you could miss your own state's much earlier cutoff entirely.

> [!WARNING]
> **Don't wait until spring to check this.** Ask your chapter adviser for your specific state and district dues deadline in September or October 2026, not in February 2027. By the time March 1 rolls around nationally, several states have already closed their own earlier registration windows.

## NLC 2027: What's Different About Columbus

FBLA selected Columbus, Ohio for the 2027 National Leadership Conference back in 2023, well ahead of the standard multi-year advance-booking cycle major student conferences use. A few practical notes if you're aiming to qualify:

- **Dates:** June 23–26, 2027 (compare to 2026's June 29 – July 2 window in San Antonio - note the 2027 conference sits about a week earlier in the calendar, which matters if your family is planning summer travel around it).
- **Venue:** Greater Columbus Convention Center.
- **Following years, for context:** NLC 2028 is set for Anaheim, California, and NLC 2029 for Indianapolis, Indiana - useful if you're a freshman this year mapping out which conferences you might reach as you move up through FBLA.

> **Source:** [New York State FBLA - NLC](https://www.nysfbla.org/conferences/nlc/) | [Pennsylvania FBLA - National Leadership Conference](https://www.pafbla.org/national-leadership-conference/) | [Illinois FBLA - National Leadership Conference](https://www.ilfbla.org/national-leadership-conference.html)

## A Month-by-Month Plan for the 2026–2027 Season

**September–October 2026 (now):** Confirm your actual dues deadline with your adviser - don't assume March 1. Start working through the 8 knowledge areas at a relaxed pace; there's no test pressure yet, which makes this the best time to build vocabulary.

**November–December 2026:** Most region/district-level competitions (where applicable) happen in this window. If your state has this layer, this is your first real test under time pressure - treat it as a diagnostic, not a verdict.

**January–March 2027:** State Leadership Conference season for most states. This is also when your state will announce its specific SLC date and location if it hasn't already - watch your chapter's communications closely during this window.

**April–May 2027:** If you've qualified for nationals, this is your final full-length practice window. Run complete 100-question, 50-minute timed simulations under real conditions.

**June 23–26, 2027:** National Leadership Conference, Columbus, Ohio.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When do the 2026–2027 FBLA guidelines come out?
They were published on fbla.org and FBLA Connect in early September 2026, consistent with FBLA's stated annual pattern of releasing updated guidelines each September.

### Is the test format changing for 2026–2027?
We found no announced changes to Introduction to Marketing Concepts specifically. The 100-question, 50-minute, 9th/10th-grade-only format has held steady for multiple consecutive years. Confirm against the current PDF's footer date before you rely on this.

### When is FBLA Nationals 2027?
June 23–26, 2027, in Columbus, Ohio, at the Greater Columbus Convention Center.

### What's the membership deadline for the 2026–2027 program year?
The national floor is March 1, 2027, at 11:59 p.m. Eastern. Your actual state or district deadline may be earlier - confirm with your chapter adviser.

### Where can I find my state's 2027 State Leadership Conference date?
There's no national list - each state FBLA chapter publishes its own SLC schedule, usually by fall or winter of the prior program year. Search your state FBLA chapter's website directly.

### Should I use the 2026 study guide or this page to prepare?
Use both, for different things. This page tells you *when* things happen this season. Our [Complete Study Guide](/blog/fbla-intro-marketing-2026) covers *what* to actually study - all 8 knowledge areas with worked examples.
