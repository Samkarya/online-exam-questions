## Is the Deadline Actually Going to Be Extended?

Maybe. Here's what actually happened the last few times CAT's deadline came up for debate:

| Cycle | Original Deadline | What Happened |
|:---|:---|:---|
| CAT 2025 | September 13, 2025 | Extended to **September 20, 2025** (about a week) |
| CAT 2024 | — | No extension |
| CAT 2023 | — | No extension |
| CAT 2022 | — | Extended to **September 27, 2022** |

So it's happened in roughly half of the last four cycles — not rare, but not something to plan a strategy around either. IIM Indore has given no signal either way for CAT 2026, extensions are never pre-announced, and there's no published trigger (server load and last-minute registration volume seem to be the informal drivers, going by past patterns).

> [!WARNING]
> Treat September 15 as final. If IIM Indore does extend, it will appear as a notice directly on **iimcat.ac.in** — not as a screenshot on a Telegram group or a coaching institute's Instagram story. Don't let an unverified "extension confirmed!" post talk you out of registering on time.

## Your Last-Mile Checklist

If you're registering in the final stretch, this order avoids most of the common failure points:

1. **Gather documents before you open the form.** You'll need a passport-style photo (white background), a scanned signature, and your category certificate if applicable. Upload failures usually come from the wrong file size or format, not the wrong document — check the exact specs in the CAT 2026 information bulletin before you start.
2. **Enter academic details from your actual marksheets**, not from memory. Small mismatches in Class 10, Class 12, or graduation percentages are a common source of correction-window scrambles later.
3. **Rank your 5 test-city preferences by genuine convenience**, not by guessing which city is "less crowded." Allotment logic isn't published, so there's no strategic edge to gaming the order — pick where you'd actually want to travel on exam day.
4. **Pay during off-peak hours if you have the choice** — early morning or midday, not the last few hours before the deadline. Slowdowns and payment-gateway timeouts cluster hard in the final 48 hours of any registration window this size.
5. **If a payment fails but money leaves your account, don't pay again right away.** Check your application status first and follow the official payment-resolution process on the portal. Panic re-payments during a glitch are a recurring, avoidable problem every cycle.
6. **Download and save your confirmation page** the moment you're done — don't rely on the confirmation email alone reaching you in time.

> [!TIP]
> None of this is exotic advice — it's the same six steps every cycle. The only real variable is whether you do them with ten days of buffer or ten minutes of buffer. Buffer wins every time.

## What If You Miss the Deadline?

There's no late window, no manual submission, and no appeals process — once September 15, 2026 passes, CAT 2026 registration is closed for good. Realistically, you have two paths from there:

**Wait for CAT 2027.** CAT runs every November, so missing 2026 costs you a year, not a shot. Used deliberately, that year buys a full, properly-paced prep cycle instead of a compressed one — which tends to show up in the score.

**Apply to an MBA entrance exam still open this admission cycle.** A few national-level options run later in the same season:

| Exam | Registration Typically Runs Through | Exam Window |
|:---|:---|:---|
| XAT | Early January | January |
| SNAP | Late November | December (up to 3 attempts) |
| NMAT by GMAC | Often into December | October–December (up to 3 attempts) |
| CMAT | Late December / January | January |

These open the door to a different, though still substantial, set of B-schools — see the full breakdown in the **[CAT 2026 Complete Guide](/blog/cat-2026-complete-guide)**. None of them substitute for CAT if an IIM is the specific goal, but they keep your MBA admission cycle alive this year instead of losing it outright.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is September 15, 2026 definitely the last date for CAT 2026 registration?
Yes, per the official notification. IIM Indore has announced no change to this date as of this update.

### Has IIM ever extended the CAT registration deadline before?
Yes — CAT 2025's deadline moved from September 13 to September 20, 2025 (about a week), and CAT 2022 was extended to September 27. CAT 2023 and CAT 2024 were not extended. There's no reliable way to predict which pattern CAT 2026 follows.

### Can I register after September 15 by paying a late fee?
No. CAT has no late-fee option, no offline submission, and no manual override. Once the portal closes, it closes for every candidate, without exception.

### What should I do if my payment fails during registration?
Don't submit a second payment immediately. Check your application status and payment confirmation first, and follow the official payment-resolution process on iimcat.ac.in — retrying immediately during a glitch is what usually causes duplicate-transaction headaches, not the original failure.

### I missed the deadline. Can I still apply to any MBA program this year?
Yes. XAT, SNAP, NMAT, and CMAT all run later in the same admission cycle and accept a wide range of B-schools, though not the IIMs specifically. Otherwise, CAT 2027 will be the next opportunity at an IIM seat.
